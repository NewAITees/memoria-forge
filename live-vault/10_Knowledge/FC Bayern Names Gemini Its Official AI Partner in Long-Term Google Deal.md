# FC Bayern Names Gemini Its Official AI Partner in Long-Term Google Deal

## Overview
FC Bayern Munich has entered into a long-term partnership with Google, designating Gemini as its official AI partner. The agreement, effective from the 2026-27 season, positions Gemini as a key part of Bayern's digital strategy, with integration across fan engagement, content creation, and supporter services. Google Pixel phones are also named as the club's official smartphone partner.

## Details
- **Partnership Scope**: The deal covers both Bayern’s men’s and women’s teams, with Gemini and Google Pixel integrated into digital content, matchday experiences, and supporter-facing services.
- **Gemini Integration**: Gemini will be used to provide real-time information on opponents, tactical analysis, statistics, line-ups, and match developments. The AI will support both in-person and remote fans.
- **Google Pixel Role**: Google Pixel will serve as the official smartphone partner, with weekly behind-the-scenes content and themed digital features like wallpapers and app icons.
- **Content Collaboration**: Content will feature players such as Jamal Musiala and Giulia Gwinn, with exclusive access to both men’s and women’s teams.
- **Digital Features**: Pixel users will gain access to Bayern-themed digital content through Pixel Drops, including personalized wallpapers and app icons.
