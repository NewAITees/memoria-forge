# State of Open Models Summer 2026 Observations

## Overview
This report summarizes key observations about the open-source model ecosystem as of mid-2026, based on data from January to August 2026. It highlights trends in model development, adoption, and the evolving balance between openness and safety.

## Key Findings

### 1. Rapid Advancement in Model Size and Performance
- Chinese labs significantly outpaced U.S. labs in terms of model size and performance. In almost every month of 2026, the largest and most performant open model from a Chinese lab exceeded the largest models from U.S. labs.
- China's monthly ceiling for model parameters ranged between 754B and 2.78 trillion, while U.S. models remained under 130B in five of seven months, with exceptions being NVIDIA's Nemotron 3 Ultra (561B) and the Inkling model from Thinking Machines Lab.
- Labs like Moonshot, MiniMax, Xiaomi, and Z.ai focused on models above 70B parameters, making their models inaccessible to most developers without quantization tools.
- Tencent and Alibaba Qwen covered a broader range, from under 1B to larger models.

### 2. Community-Driven Quantization and Accessibility
- The community's quantization layer allows developers to run large models within days, reducing the need for labs to release smaller models to gain traction.
- This has shifted the size profile of models from a measure of capability to a statement of intent, with some labs focusing on benchmark performance and others aiming to become the standard for developers.

### 3. Growth of Open-Source Ecosystem
- Public model repositories on Hugging Face (HF) grew from 2.43 million to 2.96 million, datasets from 711,000 to 1 million, and Spaces from 1.00 million to 1.44 million.
- The distribution of model downloads is highly skewed, with 85.6% of models having fewer than 200 lifetime downloads, while 1.5% of repositories account for 99.2% of all downloads.

### 4. Unsloth and Qwen Derivatives
- Unsloth, a community account, has become a major source of quantized and fine-tuning-ready models, contributing to the growth of the open-source ecosystem.
- Qwen derivatives have proliferated, indicating the model's adaptability and utility across various applications.

### 5. Openness and Governance
- The report emphasizes the role of openness in accelerating experimentation, collaboration, and the democratization of AI capabilities.
- However, it also acknowledges the challenges of ensuring safety, reliability, and governance in an increasingly open landscape.

## Unrelated Scientific Discovery
- A study published in *The Astrophysical Journal Letters* on July 27, 2026, reports the discovery of a dormant supermassive black hole located far from the center of its host galaxy, marking the first time such an object has been found at such a distance.
- The black hole was detected when it tore apart a passing star, producing a brief but powerful burst of light. This discovery has implications for understanding how galaxies and their black holes merge and evolve over time.

## Conclusion
The Summer 2026 State of Open Models highlights the rapid growth and diversification of the open-source model ecosystem, while also underscoring the need to balance openness with governance and safety. As AI models continue to proliferate, the report serves as a guide for developers, researchers, and policymakers navigating the evolving landscape of open AI.
