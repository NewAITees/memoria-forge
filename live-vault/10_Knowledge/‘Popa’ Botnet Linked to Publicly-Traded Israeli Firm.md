overview:
  The Popa botnet, a large-scale Android-based botnet, has been linked to Alarum Technologies Ltd, a publicly-traded Israeli firm. The botnet is associated with NetNut, a residential proxy service provider.
details:
  - The Popa botnet has been active for over four years, using Android TV boxes to create a residential proxy network for malicious traffic relay.
  - Researchers from multiple security firms have concluded that the botnet is tied to NetNut, operated by Alarum Technologies Ltd [NASDAQ: ALAR].
  - The botnet's purpose is to provide a persistent communication layer for encrypted connections and data relay, rather than traditional DDoS attacks.
  - The botnet is part of the Vo1d botnet, targeting unofficial Android-based TV boxes.
  - These devices are often pre-installed with proxy software, turning them into "residential proxies" that can be used for data scraping and fraud.
  - Alarum Technologies acquired NetNut, and its former employee Moishi Kramer is linked to the company's development.
