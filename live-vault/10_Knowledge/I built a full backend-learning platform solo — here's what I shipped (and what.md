# I built a full backend-learning platform solo — here's what I shipped (and what broke along the way)

## 概要

The author developed a backend-learning platform called "The Backend Institute" to address the gap between learning and practical application. The platform includes hands-on projects, a 24-topic progress roadmap, and an in-browser MongoDB setup. The goal is to help learners transition from passive learning to real-world coding skills.

## 重要な機能

- **Hands-on projects**: Designed to help learners apply their knowledge in real-world scenarios.
- **24-topic progress roadmap**: A structured learning path to guide users through the material.
- **In-browser MongoDB setup**: A tool to help learners work with databases without needing to set up a local environment.

## 主な課題

- **Technical Challenges**: The platform required significant development work, including setting up the in-browser MongoDB and ensuring the user experience was seamless.
- **User Engagement**: The author faced the challenge of keeping users engaged throughout the learning process.

## 今後の展望

- **Expanding content**: The author plans to add more topics and projects to the platform.
- **Community building**: Creating a supportive community for learners to share experiences and support each other.
- **Monetization**: Exploring ways to monetize the platform while keeping the learning experience accessible.
