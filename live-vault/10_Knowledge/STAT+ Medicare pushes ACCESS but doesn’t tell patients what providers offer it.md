### Medicare pushes ACCESS but doesn’t tell patients what providers offer it

**created**: 2026-08-11
**updated**: 2026-08-11

**Overview**:
Medicare's initiative, ACCESS, aims to improve patient access to healthcare services, but patients are not being informed about which specific providers or platforms offer the program. This lack of transparency raises concerns about how patients can effectively utilize the initiative to access care.

**Details**:
- **ACCESS Initiative**: Medicare's ACCESS program is designed to enhance access to healthcare services for beneficiaries. However, the program does not provide patients with clear information on which local providers or platforms are participating.
- **Transparency Issues**: Patients currently lack visibility into the specific providers or platforms that are part of the ACCESS program, which could hinder their ability to make informed healthcare decisions.
- **Health Tech and Regulation**: The initiative is part of broader discussions around health technology and regulatory frameworks, including the role of AI in healthcare.
